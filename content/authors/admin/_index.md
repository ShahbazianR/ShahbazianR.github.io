Reza Shahbazian is an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) at the Department of Humanities, University of Palermo. He holds the Italian National Scientific Qualification (ASN) for the role of Associate Professor in Computer Engineering.

His research focuses on artificial intelligence for real-world problems, including machine learning algorithms for IoT, localization, human activity recognition, logistics, and healthcare.
