About Me

I am an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) at the Department of Humanities, University of Palermo. I hold the Italian National Scientific Qualification (ASN) for the role of Associate Professor in Computer Engineering.

My research focuses on computer science-related topics, specifically on theoretical and practical aspects of artificial intelligence for real-world problems. With the expansion of data-driven decision-making and AI, I have been researching machine learning algorithms and techniques for applications like IoT, localization, human activity recognition, logistics, and healthcare. Currently, I am exploring new topics on explainable AI and integrating domain knowledge into neural networks.
